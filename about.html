About Me

who_i_am

Business Analytics & AI

I'm a Business Analytics professional with experience at the intersection of business operations, data, and technology.

My work focuses on turning real‑world business data into actionable insights and practical solutions. I have experience working with data analytics, business intelligence, dashboards, and reporting, and I've recently expanded my work into AI application development.

I enjoy building solutions that connect business problems with technology — from analyzing and visualizing data to developing AI‑powered applications that make information easier to access and use.

My recent work includes building an end‑to‑end AI analytics application using Python, SQL, APIs, LLMs, RAG, Azure, evaluation, and observability. The project allowed me to explore how AI can be applied to real business data while considering not only the model itself, but also accuracy, reliability, deployment, and monitoring.

I'm particularly interested in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, AI Applications, and Analytics Engineering, and I'm continuing to develop my skills in cloud technologies and modern AI systems.
