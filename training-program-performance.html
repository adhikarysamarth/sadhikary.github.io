Training Program Performance

Tracks training program outcomes and enrollment patterns — live view of the underlying Power BI report below.

overview

What this is

An executive dashboard built for a workforce training provider to track enrollment, completion, and job placement outcomes across 9,000+ students, multiple state workforce funding boards, and 160+ training programs. It replaced a manual, spreadsheet-based reporting process with a live, filterable view that career center coordinators and leadership use to monitor performance by fiscal year, funding source, state, coordinator, and program.

the_problem

Business problem

The organization receives funding from a mix of state workforce boards — WIOA across 11 states, Section 30, VRRAP, Trade Adjustment Assistance, and others — each of which reports on a July–June fiscal year rather than the calendar year. Leadership needed a single view that could:

  • Track enrollment and completion trends on the funders' fiscal calendar, not the calendar year
  • Show completion and placement rates without over- or under-counting students still mid-program
  • Break down revenue and outcomes by funding source, state, career center, and coordinator
  • Replace a manually rebuilt monthly Excel report with something that updates itself
data_pipeline

Data source & process

Student enrollment, program, and placement records were exported from the school's Student ERP system as a raw Excel extract — one row per student enrollment, covering fields like course package, admission and placement status, funding source, dates (inquiry, admission, start, end), and placement outcome.

etl_pipeline.py
01 — extraction: ERP export → raw .xlsx (student, program, funding, placement fields)
02 — transform (pandas): dedupe columns, split funding source into program + state, convert salary bands to numeric midpoints
03 — fiscal logic: derive Enrolled FY / Exited FY from Start & End Date on a July–June calendar
04 — funnel timing: inquiry→admission, admission→start, program duration (days)
05 — model (Power BI): star schema — Fact_Students + Dim_Date, Dim_Program, Dim_State, Dim_Funding, Dim_Coordinator, Dim_LeadSource
06 — refresh: re-run on new ERP export → reload → relationships & DAX measures recalculate
design_decisions

Key design decisions

  • Fiscal-year-first design. Every date-based visual is built around the funders' July–June fiscal year rather than the calendar year, including a custom sort order so month names display in fiscal sequence.
  • Rate measures exclude in-progress students. Completion rate and placement rate are calculated against students who've reached a final outcome (completed or dropped out), not the full population — so students still mid-training don't artificially dilute the numbers.
  • Cross-filterable by funding source, state, career center, coordinator, and program, so a single dashboard serves both an executive rollup view and a drill-down tool for individual funding board reporting.
the_dashboard

What it shows

  • KPI summary: total students, completion rate, placement rate, total revenue, students currently in training
  • Revenue by funding source, showing the mix across WIOA, VRRAP, Section 30, and other boards
  • Placement outcomes by program, ranking which training tracks lead to the most job placements
  • Monthly enrollment trend, in fiscal-year order, filterable by state, funding source, coordinator, program, and lead source
stack

Tools

Python (pandas) for data cleaning and fiscal-year logic · Power BI (Power Query, DAX, data modeling) for the dashboard · Excel for the intermediate cleaned data model.
